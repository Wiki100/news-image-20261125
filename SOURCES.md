# Sources — 25 November 2026

Every story in `entry.png` was checked against reporting dated
25 November 2026. Headlines and summaries in the image are paraphrases
of what those reports said; nothing was invented.

## 1. Space — Shenzhou-24 lifts off with Pakistan's first astronaut

- China's Shenzhou-24 launched on 25 November 2026 at 00:18 UTC
  (08:18 Beijing time) from Jiuquan, carrying Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi and
  Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali to the Tiangong
  station. Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first foreign
  astronaut to visit Tiangong. The spacecraft docked with Tiangong at
  11:56 Beijing time the same day.
- Reuters, "China's Shenzhou-24 space flight takes off with Pakistani
  national as first foreign astronaut":
  https://www.reuters.com/science/chinas-shenzhou-24-space-flight-takes-off-with-pakistani-national-first-foreign-2026-11-25/
- China Daily Asia, "China's Shenzhou XXIV spaceship docks with
  Tiangong space station":
  https://www.chinadailyasia.com/hk/article/643205

## 2. Markets — Asian stocks slide as the AI rally fades

- Asian stocks fell on Wednesday 25 November 2026 as a tech-led rally
  faded and fresh US tariff threats hit sentiment. Japan's Nikkei fell
  as much as 2.9%, South Korean shares fell as much as 5.3%, and Nvidia
  gave back about 6% after Jensen Huang's return as CEO.
- Reuters, "Stocks retreat as AI rebound stumbles, tariff threats loom":
  https://www.reuters.com/world/china/global-markets-global-markets-2026-11-25/

## 3. Middle East — Houthis say they hit a Saudi oil refinery

- Yemen's Houthis said on Wednesday 25 November 2026 that they had
  attacked Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura oil refinery and Riyadh's financial
  district. Saudi civil defence said 11 people were wounded on Tuesday
  in a strike on a tower in Riyadh's financial district. The wording in
  the image ("say they attacked") follows the reporting: the attack is
  the Houthis' claim.
- Reuters, "Yemen's Houthis say they attacked Saudi oil refinery and
  Riyadh financial district":
  https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/yemens-houthis-say-they-attacked-saudi-oil-refinery-riyadh-financial-district-2026-11-25/

## 4. Americas — Cartagena siege ends: all hostages freed

- Colombian special forces stormed an Éxito supermarket in Cartagena
  early on Wednesday 25 November 2026, ending a siege of almost
  20 hours and freeing all the remaining hostages. One gunman was
  killed and four were detained. The siege began on Tuesday when armed
  men, after a failed attempt to rob an armoured cash vehicle, took
  about 30 shoppers and workers hostage.
- Reuters, "All hostages held in Colombian supermarket freed, AFP
  reports":
  https://www.reuters.com/world/americas/all-hostages-held-colombian-supermarket-freed-afp-reports-2026-11-25/
- The Insight International, "Colombian special forces free Cartagena
  supermarket hostages, one gunman killed":
  https://theinsightinternational.com/colombia-cartagena-exito-hostages-rescued-special-forces-gunman-killed-november-25/

## 5. China — China probes a Politburo member for graft

- China's anti-graft watchdog launched an investigation of Shanghai
  party chief Chen Jining, a member of the Communist Party's Politburo,
  the fourth such inquiry into the second-highest leadership body
  since 2025.
- Reuters, "China's Politburo shrinks as graft crackdown spreads":
  https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-politburo-shrinks-graft-crackdown-spreads-2026-11-25/

## 6. Europe — Zelenskiy confirms strike on a Russian ship

- Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy confirmed that Ukraine was
  responsible for a drone strike on a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of
  Finland, while Norway, a strong supporter of Kyiv, urged caution in
  such operations.
- Reuters World News Summary, via Devdiscourse:
